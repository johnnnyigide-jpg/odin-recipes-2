Ingredients For Our Delicious Fried Rice

  • Rice (preferably long-grain parboiled rice)

  • Chicken (or beef, shrimp, or liver)

  • Vegetable oil

  • Carrots(diced)

  • Greeen peas

  • Sweet corns

  • Green beans

  • Green bell pepper

  • Red bell pepper

  • Onion

  • Garlic(powder)

  • Curry Powder

  • Thyme

  • Salt

  • Black pepper

  • Seasoning cubes or powdered form

  • Water

Steps To Cook Our Delicious Fried Rice

  1. Wash the rice and cook it in water until it is tender but still firm, then drain and allow it to cool

  2. Season and cook the chicken, then cut it into small pieces and set it aside

  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan and saute the onion, powdered garlic, carrots, green beans, green peas, sweet corn, and bell pepper for a few minutes

  4. Add the curry powder, thyme, salt, black pepper, and seasoning cubes then stir well so the vegetables are evenly coated with the seasonings

  5. Add the cooked rice and chicken to the pan, then stir thoroughly until the ingredient are well combined and heated through

  6. Taste and adjust the seasoning if neccessarry, then serve the fried rice hot
