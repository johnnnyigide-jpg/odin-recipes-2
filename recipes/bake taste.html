Your Sumptuous Cake

Ingredients Required

  • 500g (4 cups) all-purpose flour

  • 250g butter (or a mixture of butter and baking magarine)

  • 250g of sugar

  • 5-6 large eggs

  • 2 teaspoons baking powder

  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract or vanilla flavor

  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

  • 1/2 cup milk (liquid or evaporated milk)

Steps For Making Our Sumptuous Cake

  1. Preheat the oven and grease your cake pan with butter or oil, then dust it with flour

  2. Cream the butter and sugar together until the mixture is light and fluffy

  3. Add the eggs one at a time, then mix in a vanilla flavoring

  4. In a sepearate bowl, combine the flour, baking powder and salt, then gradually add them to the wet milk, alternating with the milk

  5. Mix gently until you have a smoother batter, then pour it into the prepared cake pan

  6. Bake in the preheated oven until the cake is golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean

  7. Allow the cake to cool before removing it from the pan and decorating it or serving it
